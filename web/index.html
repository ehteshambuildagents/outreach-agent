The problem

Buyers can smell a template
from the first line.

Real personalization takes an afternoon per prospect. So teams automate it — and the automation is exactly what gets ignored. The result is more volume, fewer replies, and a brand that reads like a robot.

Hours per lead

Genuine research doesn't scale by hand. One good email can eat an afternoon.

Templates that ignore

Mail-merge tokens aren't personalization. Prospects delete them on sight.

Hallucinated detail

Generic AI invents facts. One wrong claim and the trust is gone.

The approach

Every sentence is grounded
in something real.

Saqua reads the actual company, extracts verified facts with the quote that supports each one, and stops as soon as it knows enough to write. If a detail isn't on the page, it never appears in the email.

Adaptive research

Reads only as many pages as it needs — often just the homepage — then stops.

Quote-grounded

Each fact carries the exact quote it came from. No claim without a source.

One human email

Short, specific, in a founder's voice. It refuses to send anything generic.
