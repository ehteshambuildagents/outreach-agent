Privacy

A plain-language summary of how Saqua handles your data.

What we store

Conversations you create — the companies you research and the emails you draft — so you can return to them. That's it.

What we research

Only the public web pages you point Saqua at. It doesn't access private data, mailboxes, or accounts.

Secrets

API keys are read from the server environment. They're never shown in the interface, never logged, and never sent to the browser.

Third parties

Saqua sends page text and your prompts to the language-model provider that powers research and drafting, solely to produce your results.

Your control

You can delete any conversation from your workspace at any time. When self-hosted, all data remains within infrastructure you control.

This page is a summary, not a contract, and will be expanded into a full policy before general availability.
