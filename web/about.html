About

Outbound should sound like a person.

Saqua exists because the tools that scaled outbound also flattened it. We think you can have both reach and a human voice — if the research is real.

Mission

Make genuinely personal outreach possible at more than one-email-at-a-time speed — without inventing anything, and without sounding automated.

Vision

A world where the first message a company receives from you is specific, accurate, and worth replying to. Where “I researched you” is true, not a subject line.

Why Saqua exists

Real personalization takes an afternoon per prospect, so teams template it — and templates are exactly what buyers ignore. Generic AI made it faster but worse: it hallucinates detail and burns trust in one wrong claim. We wanted a third option: software that researches like a careful person and writes like a founder.

Our approach

  • Grounded, not generated. Every fact carries the quote it came from. If it isn't on the page, it isn't in the email.
  • Only as much research as needed. It stops the moment it knows enough — often after the homepage.
  • One human email. Short, specific, in your voice. It refuses to send anything generic.
  • You stay in control. Refine by chatting. Nothing is sent on your behalf.

We don't invent employees, investors, or numbers — on this page or anywhere in the product.
